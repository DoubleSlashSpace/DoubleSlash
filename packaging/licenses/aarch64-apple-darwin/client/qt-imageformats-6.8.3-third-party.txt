Qt Image Formats 6.8.3 — third-party components
================================================

What is shipped
---------------
The Qt Image Formats module's plugins (imageformats/qwebp, qtiff, qtga, qwbmp,
qicns) let chat show WebP, TIFF, TGA, WBMP and ICNS pictures. They are part of
the unmodified Qt 6.8.3 release and are covered by qt-6.8.3-source-information.txt
(LGPL-3.0, dynamically loaded, replaceable). The qwebp and qtiff plugins
contain the following libraries, built by The Qt Company from the sources
named below.

Corresponding source: https://download.qt.io/archive/qt/6.8/6.8.3/submodules/
(qtimageformats-everywhere-src-6.8.3), src/3rdparty/.
