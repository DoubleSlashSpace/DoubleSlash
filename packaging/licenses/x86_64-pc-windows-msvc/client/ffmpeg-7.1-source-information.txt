FFmpeg 7.1 — source and license information
===========================================

What is shipped
---------------
The DoubleSlash Windows bundle includes the FFmpeg 7.1 shared libraries
deployed alongside Qt Multimedia:

  avcodec-61.dll
  avformat-61.dll
  avutil-59.dll
  swresample-5.dll
  swscale-8.dll

The Qt FFmpeg media backend (multimedia/ffmpegmediaplugin.dll)
links against these libraries and is itself covered by the Qt entry.

Licence
-------
These libraries are used under the GNU Lesser General Public License version
2.1 or later (LGPL-2.1-or-later). The full licence text ships beside this file
as ffmpeg-7.1-LGPL-2.1.txt and is reproduced in the application's third-party
notices.

The shipped binaries embed the string "LGPL version 2.1 or later" together
with their configure options, which records that they were built in an LGPL
configuration rather than a GPL one. No GPL-only FFmpeg component
(--enable-gpl) is present in this configuration.

Provenance — unmodified upstream binaries
-----------------------------------------
These libraries are the FFmpeg builds distributed by The Qt Company as part of
the official Qt 6.8.3 MSVC 2022 x64 packages obtained from download.qt.io.
DoubleSlash does not build, patch, or modify FFmpeg. There are therefore no
modifications to FFmpeg to disclose.

Corresponding source
--------------------
Complete corresponding source for FFmpeg 7.1 is published upstream:

  https://ffmpeg.org/releases/ffmpeg-7.1.tar.xz
  https://github.com/FFmpeg/FFmpeg  (tag n7.1)

DoubleSlash's own sources and its modified local dependencies are published at
https://github.com/DoubleSlashSpace/DoubleSlash; the notices name the exact
revision each build came from. They do not contain FFmpeg sources, which are
obtained from the upstream locations above.

How the LGPL-2.1 obligations are met
------------------------------------
* Notice — the application's Settings > About > Third-Party Notices screen
  states that DoubleSlash uses FFmpeg under the LGPL-2.1-or-later and displays
  this text and the full licence.
* Licence text — ffmpeg-7.1-LGPL-2.1.txt accompanies the distribution.
* Source availability — the upstream locations above are published in the
  notices screen and in this file.
* Relinking — FFmpeg is dynamically linked as the separate DLLs listed above.
  A recipient may replace any of them with their own build of the same major
  version. See relinking-instructions.txt.
* No modifications — as recorded above, FFmpeg is shipped unmodified.
